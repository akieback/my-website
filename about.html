I'm a machine learning researcher focused on speech and audio. My work spans self-supervised representation learning, where models learn the structure of speech from huge amounts of unlabeled audio, and applied work that uses the voice to detect signs of depression and other mental health conditions.

I like turning open questions into systems that actually run: compact architectures, careful evaluation, and training at scale on clusters like a DGX B200. I'm fluent in Python and PyTorch and comfortable everywhere from the math to the data pipeline.

Before research I worked in industry at Carl Zeiss and SANDAG, which taught me to care about stakeholders, clean data, and shipping.
