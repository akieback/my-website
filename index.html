About

Machine learning for speech and health.

I'm a machine learning researcher at the James Silberrad Brown Center for Artificial Intelligence in San Diego. I study how models can learn from speech with few or no labels, and how the voice can help us understand mental health.

Recently that has meant self-supervised speech representations with Yann LeCun and Ravid Shwartz-Ziv, explainable depression detection from voice recordings, and an AI music co-creation tool we presented at NeurIPS 2025.

I care about methods that hold up outside the benchmark: compact architectures, careful evaluation, and training at scale.
